You Will Not Die Again

The third time he died was the last. His family made sure of it. They could no longer bear another death.

His first death came when he was a child. On his first day of school, his teacher found him lying in the playground, purple and rigid, his face covered in saliva that had run from his mouth and nose. When the doctor arrived, he pronounced him dead, and that same afternoon he was buried in the local cemetery. A couple of hours later, with his relatives still near the tomb, he awoke from a cataleptic seizure, and his cries could be heard blocks away. They rescued him at once, and everyone considered what had happened a true miracle. From then on, everyone knew him as Lazarus.

His second death came many years later. Lazarus had been missing for nearly a week when his supposed body was found decomposing in a sugarcane field outside the village. There seemed little doubt that it was him: the body was dressed exactly as he had been when he left home, including his cap and prescription glasses.

A week after the burial, Lazarus reappeared dressed in rags, staring blankly into the distance and speaking incoherently. The same police who had declared him dead quickly determined that he had been drugged with scopolamine and robbed of everything, even the clothes he had been wearing. The dead man, apparently, had been the thief.

The third death left no room for doubt. It happened in his own home, his body remained under watch the entire time, an autopsy and a DNA test were performed, and his funeral lasted an entire week. Despite the immense love his family and friends had for him, they all felt relieved knowing that this time they would not have to bury him again. They said goodbye without shedding a single tear because they had already shed every one they had for him.

To make sure there would not be another burial, they cremated him and divided his ashes among themselves in small bags, as though afraid the ashes might somehow come together and bring him back to life.

Even so, whenever someone knocks at the door, they all have the feeling that it is Lazarus, returned from the beyond to die once more.
