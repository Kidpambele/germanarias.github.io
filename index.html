About

Germán A. Arias

I am a writer born in the heart of Colombia's coffee region. I write about philosophy, human nature, and the contradictions that often go unnoticed in everyday life.

I am drawn to those small situations that, when observed closely, can reveal something larger about ourselves. Sometimes I explore them through reflection; other times, through microfiction, where a few words can open up a much larger question.

I write in Spanish and English.
