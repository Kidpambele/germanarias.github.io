Slow and Steady

From a young age, she was taught to be slow, to take her time with every task, never to arrive anywhere on time. The way to succeed in life, they insisted, was to take hours to do what others accomplished in seconds. To be last, never first.

That was why she felt fulfilled when she came in second in the election to lead the kingdom of turtles.
