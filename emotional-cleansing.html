Emotional Cleansing

The therapist was unable to come to his office due to a depressive episode. When his patient arrived for her appointment, she was met by the janitor, who tried to explain the situation. She did not listen and began telling him all the details of her complicated case. Overwhelmed by so much pain, he did not say a word. She left as a whole new person.
